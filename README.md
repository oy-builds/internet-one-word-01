# The Internet in One Word: scrollhole

**Scrollhole** (scroll + rabbit hole): the endless hole every feed, thread, and autoplay chain drops you into — you came online for one thing and surfaced three hours later holding forty open tabs and no memory of the first click.

It nails the internet because falling is the one experience every corner of it shares: social feeds, video queues, comment threads, and wiki spirals are all the same hole with different wallpaper. Nobody plans to enter a scrollhole and nobody climbs out on schedule, which is exactly how the whole internet feels from the inside.
